## Embark on a Cosmic Journey

Nebula Quest is a contemplative platforming experience where you play as an explorer searching for lost artifacts of a forgotten civilization scattered across the floating islands of the Azure Nebula.

## Gameplay Mechanics

- **Gravity Manipulation**: Use the ancient "Aether Gauntlet" to shift your personal gravity and walk on ceilings or walls.
- **Environmental Puzzles**: Interact with bioluminescent flora and crystalline structures to open new pathways.
- **Atmospheric Storytelling**: The narrative is told entirely through the environment and subtle musical cues, with no explicit dialogue.

## Creative Inspiration

Inspired by titles like *Journey* and *Limbo*, Nebula Quest focuses on "feel" and atmosphere over high-intensity combat.
